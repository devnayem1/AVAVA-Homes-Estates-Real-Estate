What we will not do

Four requests we always decline.

Saying so here saves you the wait for a no.

  • ✕ Value a property from photographs We measure on site or we say nothing. A figure from pictures is a guess with a letterhead on it.
  • ✕ Argue another agency's price down We only negotiate on files we hold, where we know what the comparables actually signed for.
  • ✕ Publish a file without the papers No exceptions, no matter how good the property or how urgent the sale.
  • ✕ Sell or share your details Not with lenders, not with developers, not with anyone. Ever.

Everything else gets a reply, including the ones we cannot help with.
