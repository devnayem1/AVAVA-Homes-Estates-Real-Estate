04 / One file end to end

Deed 2026/4471

Published with the seller's consent

Rua do Século 68

Three bedrooms over two floors, listed in April at a number the street had never reached. Five months, two reductions and eleven viewings later, it signed 11.4% under the first asking price — and still 6% above the district median per m².

Priced against two comparables that had themselves sold over asking the previous autumn.

Six weeks, nine viewings, no offer. The seller took 6.1% off rather than wait out the summer.

A cash buyer opened 15% below the original figure, with completion inside six weeks.

Split the difference at €1 085 000 — the number the deed would eventually carry.

07 / Method

Where these numbers come from

Signed prices are deed figures, not estimates and not our own listings talking about themselves. Four rules govern what appears on this page.

Legal checks & notary →

  • 01 · EU

    Deed value, not agency talk

    Portuguese and Spanish figures come from the notarial deed and the land registry entry.

  • 02 · US

    Board-reported closings

    US rows are closed sales as reported to the local board, with seller concessions shown separately.

  • 03 · Both

    Addresses only with consent

    Street numbers appear where the seller agreed; otherwise the register shows the block only.

  • 04 · Both

    No cherry-picking

    Every completion in the window is in the median, including the ones that went badly.
