The filewritten by the surveyor

A second-floor flat in a 1928 block two streets from the market, restored in 2021 with the original hydraulic tiles kept and the services replaced throughout. Ninety-six square metres measured on site — four under the previous listing and eight under the deed.

Three bedrooms, two of them doubles, and a six-metre balcony off the living room facing the interior courtyard. Quiet, but no sun after two. Lift added in 1998, service charge ninety-four euros with no outstanding levies, and every alteration since 1928 consented.
