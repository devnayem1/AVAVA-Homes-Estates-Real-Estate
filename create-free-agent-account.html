What it does not give you

Four things you cannot buy here.

Read this part before applying. If any of it is a problem, the register is not the right place to advertise — and we would rather you found that out now than after ten working days.

  • ✕ A better position Ranking is by closed files and reply time. There is no paid placement, on any plan.
  • ✕ Deleting a review Reviews are tied to a completed file. You may reply to one in public; you cannot remove it.
  • ✕ Listing without papers No deed, no current permit, no measured plan — no publication. This is refused 1 862 times a year.
  • ✕ Someone else's file You list what you signed. Passing a colleague's file off as your own ends the account.
