The filethe surveyor's own words

A third-floor apartment in a 1904 building on the quiet side of Rua do Século, taken back to the brick in 2019 and rebuilt with the plasterwork copied from the surviving cornices. One hundred and thirty-two square metres measured on site, against one hundred and forty on the deed.

Three bedrooms, two facing the interior courtyard and silent after nine. South-west light from four in the afternoon. Lift added in 2006, condominium ninety-eight euros a month, no outstanding levies and no works voted for the next two years.

−8 m² Our measurement against the deed. The price per square metre is calculated on one hundred and thirty-two.
