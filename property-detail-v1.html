The filewritten by the surveyor

A second-floor flat in a 1928 block two streets from the market, restored in 2021 with the original hydraulic tiles kept and the services replaced throughout. Ninety-six square metres measured on site, which is four under the figure the previous listing carried and eight under the deed.

Three bedrooms, two of them doubles, and a six-square-metre balcony off the living room facing the interior courtyard — quiet, but it does not get sun after two. The building has a lift added in 1998 and a service charge of ninety-four euros a month with no outstanding levies.

Every alteration since 1928 is consented, including the 2021 works. The energy rating is D, which is what a block of this age gives you without touching the façade; the windows were replaced but the walls are original.

−8 m² Our measurement against the deed. The price above is calculated on ninety-six, not one hundred and four.
