Reading the sheet

Salt House is cheapest per m² — and the only one we could not measure.

Sheet 4302 comes in at €4 611 / m², thirty-eight per cent under the dearest file here, and it is the cheapest to own over five years by €108 640. Olive Court has the space and the garden, but its attic was built without consent and the roof needs work inside two years.
