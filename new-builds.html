09 / Guide

Buying before it exists

Six checks our legal desk runs on every off-plan file before we let it onto the register. Do the same before you sign.

Legal checks & notary →

  • 01 · EU

    Bank guarantee

    Every instalment must be covered by a guarantee or escrow account in your name.

  • 02 · Both

    Licence on file

    Building permit issued, not merely applied for. We publish the number.

  • 03 · EU

    Habitation licence

    Handover cannot legally happen before the licença de utilização is granted.

  • 04 · US

    Builder warranty

    Ten-year structural, two-year systems, one-year workmanship — in writing.

  • 05 · Both

    Penalty clause

    A delay clause with a real number in it, not a vague best-efforts sentence.

  • 06 · Both

    Finish schedule

    The specification annexed to the contract, brand by brand, not 'or similar'.
