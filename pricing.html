The small printSheet 03 · terms in plain words

  1. 01 When is the commission due? On the day the deed is signed, deducted from the completion funds. Nothing is invoiced before that.
  2. 02 What if it does not sell? You pay nothing. Withdraw the file at any time — there is no notice period and no exit fee.
  3. 03 Is VAT on top? The 1.4% is quoted excluding VAT, which is added at the local rate. The rate for your country is shown before you sign.
  4. 04 Do buyers pay anything? No. No search fee, no viewing fee, no buyer-side commission. Buyers pay their own notary and taxes, as always.
  5. 05 What does verification cost? Nothing. The deed check, permit check, price history and the measured floor plan are all included, whether or not the property sells.
  6. 06 Can I switch from an agency? Yes, once the existing mandate has run out or been released. We will read the contract with you and say plainly what it allows.
