2 of eight chosen.

You almost certainly have the first three in a drawer already. We check them against the registry before the listing goes live, so a buyer's notary never finds something you did not know about.

  • ✓ Your deedEscritura — the notary gave you a copy when you bought Required
  • ! Land registry extractCaderneta predial — free from the tax office online Required
  • ! Energy certificateUnder ten years old. About €150 if you need a new one Required
  • ! Measured surveyWe send a surveyor. Free with an agent, €180 on your own Strongly advised

Your home cannot go on the register until the deed, the registry extract and the energy certificate are on file.

Taking them yourself

Daylight, curtains open, lights off. Stand in the corner of the room, not the doorway. Tidy the surfaces and photograph the room you like best first — it is the one buyers will see in the search results.
