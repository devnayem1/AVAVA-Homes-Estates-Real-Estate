  • Field rgba(16,15,12,0.5) Inputs and textareas.
  • Accent hover #e8b563 Gold button under the pointer.
  • Negative #c4573c Costs more, sign out, close account.

The ink ladderrgba(236,229,216, α)

  • 0.09 Table row dividers
  • 0.14 Section rules, inner panel rules
  • 0.16 Grid gaps, note-box borders
  • 0.2 Panel borders, input borders
  • 0.28 Outline buttons
  • 0.4 Micro labels, sheet codes
  • 0.5 Secondary meta, sub-labels
  • 0.6 Table cells, body values
  • 0.72 List labels, quiet body

Use these steps and no others. An in-between value is always a mistake — nine steps cover everything on the site.

Accent washesrgba(217,160,74, α)

  • 0.06 Table row hover
  • 0.08 Selected row in a picker
  • 0.10 Callout panel, the one that matters
  • 0.14 Active chip fill
  • 0.55 Active chip border
  • 0.92 Badge fill over a photograph

What the three signal colours mean

Green is always better for the person reading.Gold is the thing to look at.Orange is a cost, a delay or a gap. Never colour something green because it is large.

Nothing goes below 8px, and 8px is reserved for uppercase tracked labels. Body copy never below 11px. Uppercase always carries tracking — 0.1em at the tightest, 0.22em for labels. The 5 rows marked "floor" ship at 12px rather than the 8 and 10 named here: Envato rejects type below 12, so the template raised its floor and this guide follows the template.

03 / Spacing and geometry

03 / Spacing and geometry the rules that make pages feel the same

The measurements

  • Page gutterclamp(28px, 3.2vw, 56px)
  • Between sectionsclamp(26px, 3.4vh, 38px)
  • Panel padding22–28px
  • Table row14–15px 20px
  • Table header12px 20px
  • Column gapclamp(20px, 2.2vw, 30px)
  • Dashboard rail236px

Border radius: 0

AB

Square, everywhere. The only round things on the site are avatars, status dots and the timeline dots — where roundness means "a person" or "a point in time", never decoration.

No shadows

One exception on the whole site: the nav dropdown. Depth comes from hairlines and panel tints, not blur.

Motion

0.2s ease on hovers and state changes. 0.42s cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.61, 0.36, 1) for anything that changes size, like the gallery accordion.

Nothing animates on entry. No fades, no slide-ins, no counting numbers. A page is either drawn or it is not.

Reduced motion

prefers-reduced-motion drops every transition to 0s. Behaviour stays, animation goes.

04 / The drawing-sheet motifs

04 / The drawing-sheet motifs what makes it look like AVAVA and not a portal
  • Blueprint grid rgba(236,229,216,0.045) hairlines at 26–36px. Behind drawings and card photographs, never behind text.
  • Crop marks Four 16px corner brackets, 1px gold at 0.6, inset 14px. Only on hero drawings and featured sheets.
  • The veil hatch Means "nobody has been inside" or "not published yet". Data-driven — never decorative.
  • Plot and footprint Dashed boundary, gold hatch fill. The block is sized by sqrt(coverage) — it is a plan, not a bar.

The sheet strip

Riba Nova · Phase IIRender 1:200EU · Off-plan

Three items under a drawing, hairline above, last one gold. Identity, scale, status — in that order.

The shared hairline

Card grids never border their cards. The grid gets gap: 1px over a 0.16 background inside a matching border, and the cards paint solid. One hairline per edge, none doubled at the intersections.

05 / Buttons

05 / Buttons four kinds, and that is all
  • Primary Hover → #e8b563
  • Outline Hover → gold border and text
  • Small, in a row Inside table rows and lists
  • Destructive Never filled, never the default focus
  • Done state An action that has been taken
  • Text link Always ends with →, always gold

One primary button per view. If two things look equally important, neither is. Labels say what happens — "Book a viewing", not "Submit"; "Send the owner this case", not "Confirm".

11 / Writing

11 / Writing the part most systems leave out
  • Say the consequence "You will find out about a price cut when you next open the site — which is usually too late." Not "notifications disabled". Every setting explains what happens, not what it is called.
  • Admit what is unknown "Nobody has been inside. Budget for the worst room you cannot see." A gap in the data is content, not an omission. The hatch, the "unseen" rows and the exterior-only labels all exist for this.
  • Argue against yourself "This cut costs the owner €31 000 and reaches nobody new. Walk away." The design is allowed to talk a user out of something. That is what makes the rest of it believable.

Numbers

Thousands separated by a space Thousands separated by a space — €1 225 112, 24 128 listings. Never a comma, never a dot. One formatter for money and counts alike, or the two drift apart. Whole units unless the decimal carries meaning.

Sentences

Sentence case everywhere except tracked labels. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no "Oops". Numbers written out when they are prose ("nine files"), as figures when they are data. British spelling.
