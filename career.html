Work on the register.

Seven open roles across four countries — surveyors, engineers and the people who check the papers.

  • 38Cities
  • 24 128Listings
  • 1 940Agents

Working hereno sales floor, no cold calling

Nobody here sells houses.
We check them.

The register is not a feed of adverts — every file on it was measured, its deed read and its permits pulled before anyone saw a price. That work is done by people, and those people are what we hire.

A surveyor here walks about eleven properties a week and disagrees with the owner's stated area on roughly one in five. A documents officer reads forty deeds and refuses six. Those two numbers are the whole business.

The trades below are hired on the same terms in all four countries: a local permanent contract, no commission, and a salary band published in the listing rather than negotiated in the dark.

If you want commission and a target board, this is the wrong company. If you like being the person who noticed the extension had no permit, read on.

How hiring runsStep 01 / 04 · Day 0

Nineteen days, four steps.

1 in 9 Applicants get an offer

  1. 01 · Day 0 You apply Five fields and one question about something you checked and found wrong.
  2. 02 · Day 2—4 One call Forty minutes with the person you would report to. No panel, no HR screen first. Most people stop here
  3. 03 · Day 5—12 A real file A property from the register with something wrong in it. Paid, and you keep the day rate either way. Second most common stop
  4. 04 · Day 13—19 Answer Offer or refusal in writing, with the reasons and the notes from the task.

What you get

Paid for, not promised.

  • €2 400 A year for certification and licence fees, paid up front
  • 2 of 7 Roles are remote within the EU; the rest are on site by necessity
  • 100% Travel to surveys paid, including your own vehicle at €0.42 / km
  • 28 days Holiday plus local public days, and nobody counts your hours
  • €600 Equipment budget you choose yourself, refreshed every three years
  • 1 day A month for your own reading — the register is a research job

What you do not get

Four things we will not offer you.

  • ✕ Commission or bonuses Nobody here is paid per file, per sale or per lead. The salary is the salary.
  • ✕ A sales target There is no board on the wall. You are measured on volume checked and errors caught.
  • ✕ Weekend viewings Viewings are the agents' work, not ours. Saturdays are Saturdays.
  • ✕ Equity in place of pay We are not a startup and we will not offer you options instead of money.
